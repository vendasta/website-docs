### Overview

WordPress Hosting Pro is fully managed, secure, high-performance hosting. Every site is protected by a built-in firewall and DDoS protection, runs in its own isolated environment, and includes free SSL that renews automatically. WordPress Hosting Pro and Premium also include a global CDN. This article describes the key security features that keep your WordPress website safe and fast.

### Key Security Features

**1\. Built-in firewall**

A built-in firewall protects your site from malicious traffic. It shields your website against common threats like **SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and brute-force login attempts**, ensuring a secure online experience.

**2\. DDoS protection**

WordPress Hosting Pro mitigates **Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks**, so only legitimate traffic reaches your site. This keeps your site available when traffic is high or under attack.

**3\. Rate limiting**

To prevent flooding attacks, WordPress Hosting Pro applies **rate limiting** at multiple levels. Rate limiting stops excessive requests from bots and abusive traffic from overwhelming your site.

**4\. Secure login**

Many WordPress attacks target `wp-login.php`. WordPress Hosting Pro offers **Single Sign-On (SSO)**, so you access the WordPress admin interface only through the **secure dashboard**. This adds an extra layer of protection.

**5\. Site isolation**

Each site runs in its own isolated environment, so activity on one site cannot interfere with another. This helps keep your site's performance consistent and your data separate.

**6\. Free SSL encryption**

A free SSL certificate is included and renews automatically. It **encrypts all data** exchanged between your website and your visitors.

**7\. Global CDN (Pro and Premium only)**

WordPress Hosting Pro and Premium include a global content delivery network (CDN). It speeds up your website by **delivering content efficiently to visitors around the world**. The CDN is not included with WordPress Hosting Standard.

**8\. Plugin management**

WordPress Hosting actively manages **plugin activations and deactivations** to protect security and performance. Plugins are categorized as **"good" or "bad"** based on their security impact.

### Conclusion

WordPress Hosting Pro gives you a **secure, high-performance hosting solution** with a built-in firewall, DDoS protection, rate limiting, site isolation, free SSL, and a global CDN (Pro and Premium only). Your WordPress website stays optimized for **speed, security, and reliability**.
